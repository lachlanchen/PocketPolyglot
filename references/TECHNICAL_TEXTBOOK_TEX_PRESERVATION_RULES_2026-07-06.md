# Technical Textbook TeX Preservation Rules - 2026-07-06

Use these rules for music technique, music theory, game theory, chaos theory,
nonlinear dynamics, quantum field theory, and similar textbook conversions.

## Required Preservation

- Keep figures, captions, diagrams, graphs, tables, exercises, and examples.
- Keep equations, derivations, theorem/proof blocks, numbered formulas, and
  symbol definitions as TeX math where possible.
- Keep music notation, rhythm grids, chord charts, scale-degree notation,
  fretboard diagrams, and staff fragments as structured TeX or figure assets.
- Use referenced image assets for visual diagrams; do not replace them with
  placeholder text or prose summaries.
- Preserve source order and chapter structure, but reflow prose for pocket-size
  readability.

## Validation Gates

- Compare representative pages against source-page evidence.
- Check equation/table fidelity and numbering.
- Check figures and captions appear near the relevant text.
- Inspect TeX logs for severe overfull boxes.
- Spot-check pocket-size pages for clipped formulas, diagrams, and tables.

## Public Output Caution

For modern copyrighted textbooks, public artifacts should be study notes,
terminology tables, original explanations, and newly written exercises unless a
private/local-only full conversion is explicitly intended.
